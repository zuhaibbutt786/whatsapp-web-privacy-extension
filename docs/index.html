About the creator

Built with care for real-world privacy.

Hi, I’m Zuhaib Hussain Butt — a Data Scientist and software builder based in Gujranwala, Pakistan. I care about creating simple, useful tools that solve everyday problems.

SafeWAChat started from a simple need: keeping WhatsApp Web private when working in public places, shared offices, or while presenting on a screen. The goal was never complexity — just one clear purpose: blur what should stay private until you decide to look.

I believe privacy tools should be transparent, lightweight, and respectful of your data. SafeWAChat runs entirely on your device and does not collect or send your messages anywhere.
