How it works

Simple, natural, and always under your control.

1. Content stays blurred

Messages, media, and previews appear blurred by default so nothing is visible at a glance.

2. Hover to reveal

Move your cursor over anything you want to read. It becomes clear instantly.

3. Auto-hide again

When you move away, content blurs again after a short delay you can adjust.

4. Toggle anytime

Turn privacy on or off in one click — or with a quick keyboard shortcut.
