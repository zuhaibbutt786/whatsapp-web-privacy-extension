Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

SafeWAChat is a Chrome extension that blurs WhatsApp Web content on your device for privacy. This policy explains what the extension does with data.

What we collect

We collect nothing. SafeWAChat does not send any data to our servers or to third parties. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no account required.

What stays on your device

Settings (blur preferences, selective contacts, timers, and similar options) are stored only in your browser’s local storage via the Chrome storage API. This data never leaves your computer.

Permissions

  • storage — to save your privacy settings locally.
  • activeTab — to apply blur only on the WhatsApp Web tab when needed.
  • host permission for web.whatsapp.com — required so the extension can inject the blur styles and scripts on WhatsApp Web.

We do not request access to other sites or to your WhatsApp messages beyond what is needed to apply visual blur on the page you already have open.

WhatsApp content

The extension only changes how content is displayed in your browser (blur / unblur). It does not read, store, or transmit message text, media, or contact lists beyond the minimal processing required for the blur UI.

Third-party services

SafeWAChat does not integrate with any third-party analytics, ads, or cloud services. The only external link is the optional GitHub repository for source code and updates.

Children’s privacy

The extension is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the extension after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy? See the Contact page or open an issue on GitHub.
