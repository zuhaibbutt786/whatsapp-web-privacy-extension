Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Summary

SafeWAChat does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. The extension runs entirely in your browser. No accounts, no analytics, no cloud servers.

What the extension does

SafeWAChat injects a content script into WhatsApp Web (web.whatsapp.com) that applies CSS filters and overlays to hide message text, media, and profile pictures until you hover or toggle privacy off. Settings are stored only in your browser’s local storage (chrome.storage.local).

Data we do not collect

  • Message content or chat history
  • Contact names or phone numbers
  • Usage statistics or analytics
  • IP addresses or device identifiers
  • Any data sent to external servers

Permissions explained

  • storage – Saves your blur preferences and selective contact list locally.
  • activeTab – Allows the popup to interact with the current WhatsApp Web tab.
  • host permission for web.whatsapp.com – Required to inject the privacy script.

Third parties

SafeWAChat has no third-party services, trackers, or advertisements. The only external links on this site point to the public GitHub repository.
