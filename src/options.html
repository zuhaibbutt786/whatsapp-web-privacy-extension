Quick Tips

  • Alt + X — Toggle privacy on/off instantly

  • Timed Auto-Blur re-hides content after a few seconds
  • Use Selective Contacts to force-blur or never-blur specific people/groups
  • Incognito Mode + Screen-Share Protection for maximum safety when presenting
